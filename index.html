The thesis

Nobody knows the way that is right for you.

Not because experts are stupid. Not because you should trust yourself. Because the thing you are looking for is specific — and almost everything available to buy or listen to is general.

  1. 01

    The search

    You want a specific outcome. You look in books, podcasts, courses, threads. The material is good. It is also never quite right.

  2. 02

    The mismatch

    Mass-produced advice is written for a generalized reader. Your situation is not generalized. That is a property of the medium — not a defect in you.

  3. 03

    The almost-fit

    Because it feels close, you keep going. Another book. Another framework. The gap never closes, because the form cannot close it.

The author solved their version. That is not your answer.
